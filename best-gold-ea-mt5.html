Questions people ask

Is there one best gold EA?

No. The best EA for you depends on your broker, account size, risk tolerance and how much history you can verify. Use the checks above on any EA, including this one.

Can I trust a backtest?

A backtest only shows what a rule set would have done on past data. Ask how it was made, on which broker's data, with what costs, and then compare it with live results.

What is the biggest red flag?

Promises of guaranteed or fixed monthly profit, hidden losing trades, and results shown only as screenshots.
