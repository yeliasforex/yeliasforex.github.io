Fair play

  • Never promise profit, guaranteed results or "risk-free" returns. Trading gold carries a high risk of loss.
  • Say clearly that you earn a commission, for example "I earn a commission if you buy through my link".
  • Do not buy through your own link or code, and do not use other people's accounts. Self-referrals are blocked and logged.
  • Do not use fake reviews, misleading results or spam. Use the approved material in your marketing kit.
  • Refunded sales cancel the commission. If it was already paid, it is taken off your next payout.
  • We record partner activity (logins, link changes, payouts) to keep the program fair. For customers you refer you see the first name, plan and dates, never surnames, emails, payment details or trades.
  • Treat customers' first names with respect: never contact customers about their account or payments, never ask for their details, and never share or post their names.
  • We can pause a partner who breaks these rules. Pending money from valid sales is still paid.

Questions

Do I need to be a customer?

No. You need a free account on this site so we can connect your partner dashboard to you.

Can my fans get a discount?

Yes. You decide how much of your commission to give them, up to your rate. Whatever you give is taken from your share, never from ours.

When do I get paid?

A sale becomes payable after the refund hold. Then you request a payout from your dashboard, or we pay on our monthly run. Payouts are in USDT on the TRON (TRC20) or BNB (BEP20) network.

Do I earn on renewals?

Yes. When a customer you referred renews their yearly license, you earn the renewal rate.

What if a customer asks for a refund?

The commission for that sale is cancelled. That is why sales are held before they are paid out.

Where can I ask something else?

Write to us on the contact page.
