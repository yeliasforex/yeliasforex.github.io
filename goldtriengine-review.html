You should not have to trust a stranger with a trading robot. Here is what GoldTriEngine is, what you can check yourself in minutes, and where its limits are.

What it is

GoldTriEngine is an Expert Advisor for MetaTrader 5 that trades gold (XAUUSD) with three engines, a stop loss on every trade, no martingale and no grid. It is rule-based; the AI on the YouTube channel is only the presenter and never places, changes or closes trades. See the full product page.

What you can verify

  • Live trades: the YouTube stream shows each trade, win or loss, 24 hours a day on weekdays.
  • Track record: the Myfxbook page lists account type, deposits, balance and every trade.
  • Your own test: the free backtest EA runs in your MT5 Strategy Tester with no key and no payment.

What it does not do

  • It needs MetaTrader 5 on Windows and a Fusion Markets or IC Markets Raw account; other brokers are not supported.
  • The strategy rules are private, so you judge it by results and by your own test.
