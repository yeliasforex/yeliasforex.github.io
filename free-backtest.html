3. Download the EA and run the test

Complete steps 1 and 2 to unlock the download.

Download GoldTriEngine.ex5
  1. In MT5 choose File → Open Data Folder, open MQL5 → Experts, copy GoldTriEngine.ex5 into it and refresh the Navigator.
  2. Open the Strategy Tester (View → Strategy Tester, or Ctrl+R). Choose Expert: GoldTriEngine and Symbol: XAUUSD.
  3. Set Timeframe M15, pick your date range, a $5,000 deposit and 1:100 leverage, then press Start.
  4. Compare your result with our published backtest. Results differ with the broker, the data and the dates.
