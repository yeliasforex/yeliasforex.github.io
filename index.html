On your phone

Your EA reports to you, as it happens.

GoldTriEngine sends its own notifications to Telegram, so you know what it's doing without opening MetaTrader.

  • Start and stop. A start-up message with version, engines, license status, balance and equity.
  • Offline watch. A built-in heartbeat warns you with "EA offline detected" if MT5 or the EA stops (PC asleep, internet down, VPS restart), and says "EA back online" when it recovers.
  • Every trade. Opened and closed, with the engine, direction, stop loss and result.
  • Pending orders. When an order is placed, changed or removed.
  • Risk alerts. Drawdown warnings at levels you choose, and a message whenever a risk limit blocks a trade.
  • Daily report. Trades, win rate, net result and drawdown, every evening.

Questions

Straight answers.

What is GoldTriEngine?

GoldTriEngine is an Expert Advisor (EA), a fully automated trading robot, for MetaTrader 5. It trades gold (XAUUSD) using three independent engines called Alpha, Beta and Gamma. Its trades are streamed live on YouTube 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday, with an AI host that explains each trade.

Is GoldTriEngine an AI trading bot?

No. The trading is rule-based and algorithmic: the EA decides and manages every trade by itself. AI is used only for the live stream host, which reads what the EA did and explains it in plain English. The AI never places, changes or closes a trade.

Does GoldTriEngine use martingale or grid trading?

No. Every trade has a stop loss from the moment it opens. GoldTriEngine does not use martingale (increasing size after losses) or grid trading (stacking orders without a stop).

Can I buy GoldTriEngine?

Yes. GoldTriEngine is sold as a yearly license, priced by the account size you trade: Investor (up to $5,000 deposited), Tycoon (up to $10,000), Mogul (up to $20,000) and Billionaire (no limit). A license covers one MetaTrader 5 account in US dollars. You pay in crypto, your key is created as soon as the payment is confirmed, and you can see every trade live on YouTube before you decide. See the pricing page.

Is trading gold with an EA risky?

Yes. Gold is a volatile market and any trading robot can lose money, including GoldTriEngine. Past results, simulated or live, do not guarantee future results. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

The software behind the stream

GoldTriEngine Stream Bot

GoldTriEngine Stream Bot is a small desktop program that runs on the developer's own computer and operates the GoldTriEngine live stream. It is not offered to other users, and nobody else signs in to it.

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth scope youtube.force-ssl, YouTube Data API), the bot acts on the GoldTriEngine channel only. It:

  • reads which live broadcast is active, ends it before a scheduled restart and starts the next one;
  • sets the title, description, tags, category and thumbnail of the channel's own live streams and Shorts;
  • uploads the channel's own Shorts (short clips from the live stream) and adds them to the channel's playlists;
  • reads the public live chat and posts replies and official links in it, so the AI host can answer viewers.

The bot does not access other users' channels or private data, and no data is sold or shared. Live chat messages are kept in a local log on the developer's computer for at most 30 days and are then deleted. Details are in the privacy policy. The bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

A companion tool, GoldTriEngine Shorts, uploads the same clips to the developer's own TikTok account. These tools only act on the developer's own accounts.
