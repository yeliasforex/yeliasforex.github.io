Gold EA for MetaTrader 5

A gold (XAUUSD) EA for MT5 you can check before you pay

GoldTriEngine is an Expert Advisor for MetaTrader 5 that trades gold. Three independent engines, a stop loss on every trade, no martingale and no grid. Every live trade is shown on YouTube, win or loss.

How it trades

GoldTriEngine is rule-based, not AI trading. It has three engines called Alpha, Beta and Gamma that look for different gold setups on different timeframes. The exact rules are private; the results are public. The only setting a customer enters is the license key. Position size and risk are automatic.

Built-in risk controls

  • A stop loss on every trade from the moment it opens.
  • No martingale, no grid, no averaging down.
  • Gold only (XAUUSD), one MT5 account per license.
  • Telegram alerts: EA start and stop, trades opened and closed, offline detection, drawdown levels and a daily report.

What you need

  • MetaTrader 5 on Windows, on your PC or a Windows VPS that stays on Monday to Friday.
  • A Fusion Markets or IC Markets Raw account in USD. This is required: the EA is built, tested and supported only on these two brokers. See why Raw accounts.
  • A yearly license. The plan depends on the money you deposit on that account (up to 5,000 USD, 10,000, 20,000, or no limit). Current plans.

Questions people ask

What is a gold EA?

A gold EA is an Expert Advisor: a program that runs inside MetaTrader and trades gold (XAUUSD) automatically by fixed rules, with no manual clicks.

Does GoldTriEngine use martingale or grid?

No. Every trade has a stop loss from the moment it opens, and the EA does not average down, double up or build grids.

Does it work on MT4 or on a phone?

No. It runs on MetaTrader 5 on Windows, on a computer or a VPS. Phones cannot run Expert Advisors.

Which brokers can I use?

A Fusion Markets or IC Markets Raw account (USD) is required. GoldTriEngine is built, tested and supported only on these two brokers.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Download the free backtest EA and run it in the MT5 Strategy Tester on gold. You can also watch every live trade on YouTube.
