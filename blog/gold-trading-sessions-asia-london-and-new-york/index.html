Gold Trading Sessions: Asia, London and New York

Gold trades nearly 24 hours a day from the Sunday evening open to the Friday close, but most activity comes in the London and New York sessions and especially in their overlap. Hours move by about an hour with daylight saving, and spreads usually widen when liquidity is thin, such as around the daily rollover.

YeliasPublished 13 November 20262 min read

A market that is open almost all week

Spot gold (XAUUSD) trades from the Sunday evening open until the Friday close, with a short daily break around the rollover. During that time, trading follows the sun: first Asia, then London, then New York.

SessionApproximate hours (UTC)Character
Asia (Tokyo, Sydney, Hong Kong)About 23:00 to 08:00Usually quieter; ranges can be narrower
LondonAbout 07:00 to 16:00Activity picks up; European data and a busy open
New YorkAbout 13:00 to 21:00US data and decisions; often the largest moves
London and New York overlapAbout 13:00 to 16:00Typically the most active window

What this means in practice

  • Moves cluster. Many of the biggest swings happen when major data comes out in the New York morning. See how to read NFP, CPI and other news data.
  • Quiet hours are not safe hours. Thin liquidity can mean wider spreads and sudden jumps on a headline.
  • The daily rollover is awkward. Around the end of the New York trading day, spreads often widen and some brokers pause. Avoid placing or adjusting orders there.
  • Weekends create gaps. The price at the Sunday open can differ from Friday's close.

Find your own broker's times

MetaTrader shows server time, which is set by your broker and is usually a few hours ahead of UTC. When you read a chart, know what time zone it uses. A robot that trades by the clock must use the same time as the broker's server, not your local time.

  1. Find your broker's server time in the terminal's Market Watch or on the broker's site.
  2. Mark the three sessions in that time zone on a note near your screen.
  3. List the news times for the week in the same zone.
  4. Check how your robot treats the rollover and the weekend.
Match your plan to the hours

If you cannot watch the New York session, you may prefer a strategy that does not trade through it, or a stop that does not need watching.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to trade gold?

There is no universal best time. Activity is highest in the London and New York overlap, which brings more opportunity and more risk.

Why do spreads widen at certain times?

Fewer orders are available at each price, for example around the daily rollover and in very quiet hours, so the gap between buy and sell grows.

Does daylight saving change the sessions?

Yes. Hours shift by about an hour in spring and autumn, and the US and Europe change on different dates.
