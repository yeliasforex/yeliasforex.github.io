What it means for risk

A normal full day in gold is about $53 right now (one standard daily move). Today travelled $151 from low to high, which is 2.8 times that. If your stop was $27 away, it would have been touched by the day's range alone, before any view about direction.

  • Quiet days (under half a normal move) are the minority, so a tight stop is exposed on most sessions.
  • Size is set by the stop, not the other way round. See position sizing and risk of ruin.
  • Volatility clusters. A busy day makes the next day more likely to be busy too, which is why the ranges in our daily outlooks change from day to day.

Where this sits in the last two years

Gold's range today of 3.83% compares with an average of 1.88% over the last 30 trading days and 2.50% over the last 250. Today's move of +3.40% was above the size of an average day (1.28%) over the last year.
