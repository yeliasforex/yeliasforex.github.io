Leverage and Margin on Gold: How a Margin Call Happens

Margin is the deposit the broker holds to keep a position open, and leverage sets how small it is. On a typical MetaTrader 5 account one lot of gold is 100 ounces, so a $1 move changes the profit by $100. A margin call comes when equity falls too close to the margin in use, which careful position sizing avoids.

YeliasPublished 16 November 20262 min read

What leverage really is

Leverage is the ratio between the size of the position and the money you must put up to hold it. With 1:100 leverage you need 1% of the position value as margin. With 1:500 you need 0.2%. Leverage does not change how much a price move costs you. It only changes how much of your account is tied up.

On most MetaTrader 5 brokers one standard lot of gold (XAUUSD) is 100 troy ounces. That means a $1 move in the gold price is a $100 change in profit or loss per lot. Contract sizes can differ, so always check your own broker's specification.

Margin level and the margin call

Margin level = equity / margin used x 100%. As open trades lose money, equity falls and the level drops. Brokers set a margin-call level and a stop-out level, where they close positions automatically. These levels differ between brokers, so read yours.

Take a 10,000 account with 1 lot open at 1:100, using 4,000 of margin. The margin level is 250%. If the trade loses 3,000, equity is 7,000 and the level is 175%. The account has not been wiped out, but a larger move against it would push the level toward the broker's limit.

The common mistake

Using high leverage to open a large lot because 'the margin is small'. The margin is small but the loss per dollar of price movement is not. Size the trade from your stop-loss distance and the amount you can lose, never from the margin available.

  1. Decide the loss you accept on one trade, as a percent of the account.
  2. Measure the stop distance in dollars of gold price.
  3. Divide the amount at risk by the stop distance times 100 to get the lot size.
  4. Check the margin level with all trades open at once. If it falls near the broker's limit, reduce the size.

Frequently asked questions

Is higher leverage more dangerous?

Not by itself, because risk per dollar of movement depends on lot size. But high leverage makes it easy to open a lot size far too big for the account, which is where the danger comes from.

How many ounces is one lot of gold?

On most MetaTrader 5 brokers 1.00 lot is 100 ounces and 0.01 lot is 1 ounce. Check the contract specification at your broker.

What is a stop-out?

It is the level at which the broker starts closing your positions automatically because margin level has fallen too low. The exact percentage differs between brokers.
