GoldTriEngine for MetaTrader 5

One file. The only setting is your license key from your account page.

Download GoldTriEngine.ex5 v1.08
Read this before the first start. The first MT5 account you attach your key to, demo or real, is bound to it. A bound key cannot be moved unless you contact support and pay a 100 USD unbind fee (@GoldTriEngine_Support). If you want to try it on a demo account first, that demo account is the one that gets bound. Only USD accounts are supported. MT5 will show a confirmation box naming the account before it binds.

Setup in 6 steps

  1. In MT5 choose File → Open Data Folder, open MQL5 → Experts and copy GoldTriEngine.ex5 into it. Restart MT5 or refresh the Navigator.
  2. Choose Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL and add https://ekljeyquxvlfbeadgszx.supabase.co
  3. Open a XAUUSD chart (M15 gives the best view) and drag GoldTriEngine onto it.
  4. On the Common tab tick Allow Algo Trading. On the Inputs tab paste your license key.
  5. Press OK. Confirm the account binding when MT5 asks.
  6. The panel on the chart should show License ACTIVE. If it shows a problem, it also tells you what to do. The same information is on your account page.
