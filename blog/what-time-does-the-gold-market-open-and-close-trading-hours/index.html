What Time Does the Gold Market Open and Close? Trading Hours Explained

Spot gold (XAUUSD) trades about 23 hours a day from Sunday evening to Friday evening, UTC time, with a short daily break around 22:00 UTC. The exact times depend on your broker, so check the contract specification in MetaTrader 5, and remember that daylight saving changes the local clock time twice a year.

YeliasPublished 24 November 20262 min read

The short answer

Gold quoted against the US dollar (XAUUSD) is traded by banks and brokers in every time zone, so it has no single opening bell. In practice the week opens on Sunday evening (around 22:00 to 23:00 UTC) and closes on Friday evening (around 21:00 to 22:00 UTC). Between those two points it trades continuously, apart from a short break each day.

Why the exact time depends on your broker

  • Server time. MetaTrader shows the broker's server time, which is often UTC+2 in winter and UTC+3 in summer. A chart that shows a candle at 00:00 is a server-time candle, not your local midnight.
  • The daily break. Many brokers pause gold for about an hour around the end of the New York trading day. Spreads are often wider just before and after it.
  • Holidays. Around Christmas, New Year and some US holidays, brokers close early or open late. Their notices list the dates.
  • Different instruments. Gold futures and a broker's CFD on gold can have different hours, so do not assume they match.

Converting 22:00 UTC to your time

The table converts a 22:00 UTC time (about when the week closes on Friday and opens on Sunday) into some local times. Daylight saving moves the numbers by an hour in places that use it, so treat this as a guide.

City22:00 UTC is
Singapore (UTC+8)06:00 next morning
Sydney in summer (UTC+11)09:00 next morning
Mumbai (UTC+5:30)03:30 next morning
Dubai (UTC+4)02:00 next morning
London in winter (UTC+0)22:00 the same evening
New York in winter (UTC-5)17:00 the same afternoon

When the market is busiest

Most of gold's movement happens when London and New York are both open, roughly 13:00 to 17:00 UTC, because that is when the most money is trading. The Asian hours are usually quieter, though news can change that quickly. See the Asia, London and New York sessions for how each part of the day behaves.

Check it in MetaTrader 5

Open Market Watch, right-click XAUUSD, choose Specification, and read the Trade sessions section. That is the one source that is always correct for your broker.

Frequently asked questions

Is gold traded on weekends?

Not by most brokers. The week closes on Friday evening and opens on Sunday evening in UTC time, so there is no trading in between. News on the weekend shows up as a gap when trading resumes.

What time does gold open on Sunday?

Usually around 22:00 to 23:00 UTC, but it depends on the broker. The Trade sessions entry in the XAUUSD specification in MetaTrader 5 shows the exact time.

Why does my chart not match my local time?

MetaTrader charts use the broker's server time, often UTC+2 in winter and UTC+3 in summer, not the clock on your computer.

Is there a best time to trade gold?

Activity is highest when London and New York overlap, which is also when spreads are usually tightest. Higher activity also means larger price swings, so it is not automatically safer.
