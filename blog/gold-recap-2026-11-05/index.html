What it means for risk

A normal full day in gold is about $62 right now (one standard daily move). Today travelled $89 from low to high, which is 1.4 times that. If your stop was $31 away, it would have been touched by the day's range alone, before any view about direction.

  • Quiet days (under half a normal move) are the minority, so a tight stop is exposed on most sessions.
  • Size is set by the stop, not the other way round. See position sizing and risk of ruin.
  • Volatility clusters. A busy day makes the next day more likely to be busy too, which is why the ranges in our daily outlooks change from day to day.
