AI Trading Agents vs Expert Advisors: What Is the Difference?

A classic Expert Advisor follows fixed rules written by a person, so its behaviour can be tested and repeated. An AI agent uses a model, often a language model, to decide or to act, which makes it flexible but harder to test, explain and control. Both need risk limits, and neither is a guarantee.

YeliasPublished 17 November 20262 min read

Two different things sold under one word

'AI trading bot' can mean very different things. At one end is a classic Expert Advisor: fixed rules, written down, that do the same thing every time. At the other is an AI agent: software that uses a trained model, often a large language model, to interpret information and choose actions.

What each is good at

  • Classic EAs are good at precise, repeatable execution: sizing, stops, entries by rule, 24-hour discipline.
  • AI models are good at working with text and messy information: summarising news, reading reports, drafting code. They are not good at being trusted with unchecked numbers.
  • Together, the safest pattern is to let rules handle money and risk, and let AI help with research that a person or a check can verify.

Questions to ask about any 'AI' product

  1. What exactly does the AI decide? Entry, size, exit, or only a comment?
  2. What stops a bad decision? A hard stop-loss and a hard limit on size, set outside the AI.
  3. Can the result be reproduced and tested? If not, you cannot know how it will behave.
  4. Is there a live, verified record? The word 'AI' is not evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Is an AI agent better than a classic EA?

Not by default. It is more flexible but harder to test and control. Many sound systems use no AI at all.

Can an AI agent trade gold on its own?

Technically yes, but without fixed risk limits and a verified record it is a gamble. Treat any claim of reliable AI profits with caution.

Why can an AI answer differ for the same question?

Language models generate answers with some randomness and depend on exactly how the question is phrased, which makes results hard to reproduce.
