Brokers for the gold EA

Gold EA for Fusion Markets and IC Markets Raw accounts

A robot that trades gold often depends on tight spreads, low commission and fast execution. That is why GoldTriEngine requires a Fusion Markets or IC Markets Raw account and is built, tested and supported only on these two brokers.

Why Raw accounts

A Raw (or Zero) account gives a very small spread plus a fixed commission, so the cost of each trade is clear. A standard account has no commission but a wider spread, which changes how a gold EA behaves. The published backtest used Fusion Markets data.

Set up in short

  1. Open a Raw or Zero account in USD with MT5. A demo account at the same broker is enough for the free backtest.
  2. Install MetaTrader 5 on Windows (PC or VPS).
  3. Download the free backtest EA and test it, or get a license to run it live.
