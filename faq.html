FAQ

GoldTriEngine, answered.

Everything people ask about the gold EA, the live stream and getting a license. Short answers, no sales talk.

What is GoldTriEngine?

GoldTriEngine is an Expert Advisor (EA), a fully automated trading robot, for MetaTrader 5. It trades gold (XAUUSD) using three independent engines called Alpha, Beta and Gamma. Its trades are streamed live on YouTube 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday, with an AI host that explains each trade.

Is GoldTriEngine an AI trading bot?

No. The trading is rule-based and algorithmic: the EA decides and manages every trade by itself. AI is used only for the live stream host, which reads what the EA did and explains it in plain English. The AI never places, changes or closes a trade.

Which market and platform does GoldTriEngine trade?

Gold (XAUUSD) only, on MetaTrader 5 (MT5). It is not available for MetaTrader 4 or other platforms.

Who hosts the GoldTriEngine live stream?

An animated AI robot. The GoldTriEngine YouTube channel is hosted by an AI presenter whose face reacts to each trade event and whose voice explains every entry, exit and news event, 24 hours a day on trading days. It also answers questions from the live chat. The host only explains; the EA does the trading, and the AI never tells viewers to buy or sell.

Does GoldTriEngine use martingale or grid trading?

No. Every trade has a stop loss from the moment it opens. GoldTriEngine does not use martingale (increasing size after losses) or grid trading (stacking orders without a stop).

Does GoldTriEngine send notifications?

Yes. GoldTriEngine sends its own Telegram notifications: a start-up message (version, engines, license status, balance and equity), an "EA offline detected" warning from its built-in heartbeat if MT5 or the EA stops running and an "EA back online" message when it recovers, when trades open and close (engine, direction, stop loss and result), when pending orders are placed or changed, drawdown alerts at levels you set, a message when a risk limit blocks a trade, and a daily report with trades, win rate, net result and drawdown. Each alert type can be switched on or off.

Where can I see GoldTriEngine's live results?

Every trade runs live on YouTube. The full track record, including the account type, deposits, balance and every closed trade, is on Myfxbook and in the live account section of this website.

What are GoldTriEngine's backtest results?

In the MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, GoldTriEngine v1.08 on XAUUSD from 1 January 2024 to 17 October 2026 (Fusion Markets data, $5,000 start, 1:100 leverage) made 1,302 trades with a profit factor of 2.00, 52.2% winning trades, an average win 1.84 times the average loss, a largest relative equity drawdown of 17.7%, and 29 of 34 months positive. Backtests are simulations and do not guarantee future results.

How often does GoldTriEngine trade?

In the backtest it averaged about two trades per trading day, with some days having none. It trades on weekdays, when the gold market is open.

Can I buy GoldTriEngine?

Yes. GoldTriEngine is sold as a yearly license, priced by the account size you trade: Investor (up to $5,000 deposited), Tycoon (up to $10,000), Mogul (up to $20,000) and Billionaire (no limit). A license covers one MetaTrader 5 account in US dollars. You pay in crypto, your key is created as soon as the payment is confirmed, and you can see every trade live on YouTube before you decide. See the pricing page.

How much does a GoldTriEngine license cost?

It is a yearly license, paid in advance. It includes the EA, Telegram notifications, a setup guide, personal setup help and free updates during the license. Plans start at $1,000 per year (Investor, deposits up to $5,000); see the pricing page for all four plans.

Which brokers are required?

GoldTriEngine requires a Fusion Markets or IC Markets Raw account on MT5. It is built, tested and supported only on these two brokers, because a robot that trades gold often depends on tight spreads, low commission and fast execution, which is why Raw accounts are required. Other brokers are not supported.

What do I need to run GoldTriEngine?

MetaTrader 5 on Windows, logged in to a Fusion Markets or IC Markets Raw account (required), and a computer or VPS that keeps MT5 running 24/5. The license links to the first MT5 account it runs on.

Does the AI host give trading signals?

No. The host explains trades the EA has already taken, the news that moves gold, and general trading education. It never tells viewers to buy or sell, and nothing on the stream is financial advice.

Is the GoldTriEngine strategy public?

No. The rules of the three engines are private. What is public is every result: each live trade is shown on the stream, and the track record is on Myfxbook.

Yes. Gold is a volatile market and any trading robot can lose money, including GoldTriEngine. Past results, simulated or live, do not guarantee future results. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.
